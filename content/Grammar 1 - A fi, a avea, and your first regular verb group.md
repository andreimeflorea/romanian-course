Romanian verbs are grouped into **conjugation groups** (patterns of endings).

However, the two most important verbs:

- **a fi** = to be
- **a avea** = to have

are **irregular**.

That means their forms do **not** fully follow the normal patterns.  
But they are extremely common and useful, so they must be learned first.

After that, we can learn a nice, predictable, well-behaved verb:

- **a lucra** = to work

which belongs to a regular Romanian verb group.

# Useful Vocabulary

Romanian nouns have grammatical gender.

For now, it is best to learn:
- the singular form
- the plural form
- the gender

The plural and feminine patterns are **not always fully regular**.  
Over time, however, the patterns start to feel natural and intuitive.

For now, simply take the forms as they are and learn them together as vocabulary.

| Singular | Plural | Gender | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| student | studenți | 🔵 masculine | male student |
| studentă | studente | 🔴 feminine | female student |
| băiat | băieți | 🔵 masculine | boy |
| fată | fete | 🔴 feminine | girl |
| universitate | universități | 🔴 feminine | university |

# Example Sentences with **a fi**

- Eu sunt student.  
  = I am **[a male]** student.

- Eu sunt studentă.  
  = I am **[a female]** student.

- Ea este studentă la universitate.  
  = She is **[a female]** student at **[the]** university.

- Ei sunt studenți.  
  = They are **[male/mixed]** students.

- Ele sunt studente.  
  = They are **[female]** students.

---

# Example Sentences with **a avea**

- Noi avem studenți.  
  = We have students.

- Universitatea are studente.  
  = The university has **[female]** students.

- Voi aveți o universitate.  
  = You **[plural]** have a university.

# 3. Romanian Verb Groups

Romanian verbs are traditionally divided into 4 groups based on the infinitive ending:

| Group | Ending  | Example |
| ----- | ------- | ------- |
| I     | -a      | a lucra |
| II    | -ea     | a vedea |
| III   | -e      | a merge |
| IV    | -i / -î | a vorbi |

---

# 4. **A LUCRA** — “to work”  
## A regular Group I verb

Infinitive:
- **a lucra** = to work

Stem:
- lucr-

This verb follows a predictable pattern.

---

# Present Tense of **a lucra**

| Pronoun | Verb | Ending Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| eu | lucrez | **-ez** |
| tu | lucrezi | **-ezi** |
| el/ea | lucrează | **-ează** |
| noi | lucrăm | **-ăm** |
| voi | lucrați | **-ați** |
| ei/ele | lucrează | **-ează** |

---

# Important Pattern

Notice the endings:

- eu → **-ez**
- tu → **-ezi**
- el/ea → **-ează**
- noi → **-ăm**
- voi → **-ați**
- ei/ele → **-ează**

Many Group I verbs behave similarly.

---

# Example Sentences with **a lucra**

- Eu lucrez la universitate.  
  = I work at **[the]** university.

- Ea lucrează la universitate.  
  = She works at **[the]** university.

- Noi lucrăm la universitate.  
  = We work at **[the]** university.

- Studenții lucrează.  
  = The **[male/mixed]** students work.

- Studentele lucrează.  
  = The **[female]** students work.

> **Note:** Romanian **la** often corresponds more closely to English “at the” rather than simply “at”, even though Romanian does not always use a separate word for “the” in these expressions.

# 5. Another Regular Group I Verb

## **a desena** = to draw

This verb behaves very similarly to **a lucra**.

Because it is also a regular **Group I (-a)** verb, it uses almost the same endings.

| Pronoun | Verb      | Ending Pattern |
| ------- | --------- | -------------- |
| eu      | desenez   | **-ez**        |
| tu      | desenezi  | **-ezi**       |
| el/ea   | desenează | **-ează**      |
| noi     | desenăm   | **-ăm**        |
| voi     | desenați  | **-ați**       |
| ei/ele  | desenează | **-ează**      |

## First regular verb group
- **a lucra**
- **a desena**

These are regular **Group I (-a)** verbs with predictable endings:

- **-ez**
- **-ezi**
- **-ează**
- **-ăm**
- **-ați**

Learning these patterns will make many future Romanian verbs much easier.
