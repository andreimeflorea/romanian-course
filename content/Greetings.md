
Romanian greetings are simple and commonly used in everyday conversation.

---

## Common Greetings

| Romanian | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bună! | Hi! / Hello! | Friendly and informal |
| Salut! | Hi! | Casual |
| Bună ziua! | Good day! | Polite and common |
| Bună dimineața! | Good morning! | Used until late morning |
| Bună seara! | Good evening! | Evening greeting |
| La revedere! | Goodbye! | Formal/polite goodbye |
| Pa! | Bye! | Informal goodbye |
| Pa pa! | Bye bye! | Very casual and friendly |

---

# Mini Dialogues

## Informal Greeting

**A:** Bună!  
**B:** Salut!

---

## Polite Greeting

**A:** Bună ziua!  
**B:** Bună ziua!

---

## Formal Goodbye

**A:** La revedere!  
**B:** La revedere!

---

## Casual Goodbye

**A:** Pa!  
**B:** Pa!

---

## Mixed Formal + Casual Goodbye

**A:** La revedere!  
**B:** Pa pa!

# Cultural Notes

- Romanians often use **Bună!** in casual everyday situations.
- **Bună ziua!** is polite and appropriate with strangers, older people, or in professional settings.
- **Salut!** is friendly and informal.
- **Pa!** and **Pa pa!** are common informal goodbyes.
- **La revedere!** is more formal and literally means “until seeing each other again.”
