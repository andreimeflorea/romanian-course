#Phonetics

Romanian is a very phonetic language.

In most cases:
> **words are pronounced exactly as they are written.**

Once you learn the sounds of the letters, reading Romanian becomes much easier than English or French.

---

# Romanian Vowels

| Letter | Approximate sound | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a | like *a* in *father* | casă |
| ă | like the first *a* in *about* | mamă |
| â / î | close central vowel | câine, înger |
| e | like *e* in *bed* | mere |
| i | like *ee* in *see* | mic |
| o | like *o* in *more* | om |
| u | like *oo* in *food* | urs |

# Important Pronunciation Rules

Romanian changes the pronunciation of some consonants depending on the vowels that follow them.

These are very important patterns.

---

# CE and CI

| Combination | Sound               |
| ----------- | ------------------- |
| ce          | "che" as in *check* |
| ci          | "chee"              |

Examples:

| Romanian | Approximate pronunciation |
|---|---|
| cer | cher |
| ceai | chai |
| cinci | cheench |
| cine | chee-neh |

---

# CHE and CHI

Adding **h** keeps the hard K sound.

| Combination | Sound |
|---|---|
| che | "ke" |
| chi | "kee" |

Examples:

| Romanian | Approximate pronunciation |
|---|---|
| chem | kem |
| cheie | kay-ye |
| chin | keen |
| ochi | oh-kee |

---

# GE and GI

| Combination | Sound |
|---|---|
| ge | soft "j" sound |
| gi | soft "j" sound |

Examples:

| Romanian | Approximate pronunciation |
|---|---|
| ger | jer |
| geam | jam |
| girafă | jee-rah-fuh |
| magie | mah-jee-eh |

---

# GHE and GHI

Adding **h** keeps the hard G sound.

| Combination | Sound |
|---|---|
| ghe | hard "g" |
| ghi | hard "g" |

Examples:

| Romanian | Approximate pronunciation |
|---|---|
| gheață | ghyah-tsuh |
| ghete | gheh-teh |
| ghid | gheed |
| unghi | oon-ghee |

# Special Word: oaie

## oaie

Meaning: sheep

This word is famous because it contains many vowels together.

Pronunciation:

> oh-AH-yeh

Romanian often combines vowels smoothly like this.

---

# Final Notes

- Romanian pronunciation is very consistent.
- Most letters always sound the same.
- Stress usually falls naturally and becomes easier with exposure.
- Reading out loud is one of the best ways to improve quickly.
